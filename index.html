LAYOUTS
Pinch (thumb + index) in the air to drop a point. Two points make a string. Pinch near a point to drag it — closer = higher note, and moving it while the string rings bends the pitch. Strum with open fingers. Drag a point to the ✕ to delete.

AIR STRINGS

Your camera becomes a mirror. Pinch to hang points in the air, stretch strings between them, and strum with your fingers. Short strings sing high, long strings go low. Nothing leaves your device.

No camera? You can still play with a mouse: click to place and drag points, hover across a string to strum. Turn your sound up.
